On-time performance is on-time arrivals divided by completed flights. An arrival is on time when it is less than 15 minutes late; completed flights exclude cancellations and diversions. Each row aggregates one airline's flights on one departure-arrival route. Airlines are ranked by on-time performance in one overall list for the current filters; when rates tie, more completed flights rank higher. Rank 1 appears only once. Source: U.S. DOT Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS) TranStats.
